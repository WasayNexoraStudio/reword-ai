How to rewrite an essay

RewordAI blog · October 2026

Rewriting an essay is editing, not generating a new paper. Keep your thesis. Change wording that is stiff, too close to a source, or too long. Do not ask a tool to invent the argument.

Work one paragraph at a time

A full paste mixes thesis, evidence, and conclusion. One paragraph keeps the claim under your control. Use the essay rewriter or the essay paraphraser, then stitch sections yourself.

Protect quotes and data

Quoted lines stay quoted. Numbers stay numbers. If Formal mode changes a statistic, put the original back. The tool does not know your sources.

Pick a mode for the job

  • Formal — academic or workplace register.
  • Fluency — easier reading without a stiff tone.
  • Shorten — cut filler, keep the claim.

Then edit like a human

Read the rewrite aloud. Check topic sentences. Add citations. If a paragraph still sounds unlike you, rewrite it again or start from your own outline. Related: formal vs fluent writing.

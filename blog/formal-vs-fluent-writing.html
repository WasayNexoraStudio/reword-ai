Formal vs fluent writing

RewordAI blog · October 2026

Formal and fluent are both useful. They are not the same. Formal is a register. Fluency is how easily a sentence reads. You can be formal and still hard to follow. You can be fluent and still too casual for a report.

When Formal helps

Emails to people you do not know well. Essays. Reports. Drop slang, fill in missing subjects, and avoid “gonna” or “a bunch of”. The formal tone rewriter is for this pass.

When Fluency helps

A paragraph that is true but dense. Long clauses. Repeated “due to the fact that”. Fluency aims for shorter paths through the same idea. Use it on the paragraph rewriter after you already know what you mean.

How to choose

  • School or work document → Formal first.
  • Blog post, caption, or explanation → Fluency first.
  • Still too long after either → Shorten, not another Formal pass.

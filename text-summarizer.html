Summarize vs Shorten vs paraphrase

  • Summarize — keep key points, drop detail, shorter recap.
  • Shorten — cut extra words but keep most of the original claims.
  • Paraphrase — same idea, new wording, similar length.

FAQ

What is the difference between paraphrase and summarize?

A paraphrase restates the idea in new wording. A summary is shorter and keeps only the key points.

How to paraphrase text in 3 steps

Paraphrasing means restating an idea in new wording. It is not copying with a few synonyms. Use these steps, then try the free tool below if you want a second draft.

1. Read until you understand

Do not rewrite a sentence you do not understand. Note the claim, the reason, and any numbers or names you must keep.

2. Rewrite the structure

Change the order of clauses. Split a long sentence or join two short ones. Swap the wording after the structure changes, not before.

3. Check meaning and cite

Compare your version with the original. If the idea came from someone else, add a citation. A paraphrasing tool can help with wording; you still own the editing and the sources.

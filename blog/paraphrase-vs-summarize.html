Paraphrase vs summarize

RewordAI blog · October 2026

People mix these up because both change wording. They do different jobs. Use paraphrase when you still need the full idea. Use summarize when you only need the point.

Paraphrase

Same claim, new sentences, similar length. You keep examples and reasons. Standard, Fluency, and Formal on the paraphrasing tool are for this. Shorten is closer to a tighter paraphrase than to a recap.

Summarize

Shorter. Key points only. Drop examples unless they are the point. Summarize mode on the text summarizer is built for this. It should not invent facts that were not in the original.

A quick check

  • If a teacher asked you to restate a paragraph in your own words, paraphrase.
  • If someone asked “what is this about?”, summarize.
  • If the output is as long as the input, it is not a summary.

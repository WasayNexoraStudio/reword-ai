When a paragraph rewriter helps

Use it when a paragraph is true but hard to read: too long, too stiff, or too close to a source. Fluency smooths the wording. Formal fits school or work. Shorten cuts extra words without dropping the point.

Need one line only? Use the sentence rewriter. Working on a full draft? Use the essay paraphraser. New to rewriting? Read how to paraphrase.

How to rewrite a paragraph

  1. Paste one paragraph, not a whole essay.
  2. Pick Fluency, Formal, or Shorten.
  3. Click Rewrite paragraph, then edit names, numbers, and citations yourself.

FAQ

What is a paragraph rewriter?

It restates a full paragraph in new wording while keeping the idea. It is not a one-word synonym swap.

Is this paragraph rewriter free?

Yes. No account is required.

Should I rewrite one paragraph or a whole essay?

One paragraph at a time is clearer. Then move to the essay paraphraser if you still need a longer pass.

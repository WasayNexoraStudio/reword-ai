How to paraphrase without plagiarism

RewordAI blog · October 2026

Swapping a few synonyms is still close to the original. Plagiarism is about using someone else’s idea or wording as if it were yours. A paraphrase tool does not add a citation. You do.

1. Understand the claim first

Do not rewrite a sentence you cannot explain out loud. Note the claim, the reason, and any number or name you must keep. If you cannot restate the idea without looking, you are not ready to paraphrase it.

2. Change the structure, then the words

Start with clause order. Split a long sentence or join two short ones. After the shape changes, replace the wording. That is a paraphrase. Replacing “due to the fact that” with “because” and leaving the rest is not.

3. Keep facts. Cite the source.

Dates, statistics, and unique phrasing belong to the source. Put them in quotes or keep a citation after your rewrite. RewordAI can help with fluency; it cannot know your bibliography.

4. Compare both versions

Read the original and your draft side by side. If a reader could still match them line by line, rewrite again. Then try the how to paraphrase steps on a sentence of your own, or paste one line into the sentence rewriter.

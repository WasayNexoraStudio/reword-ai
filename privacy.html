Privacy policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026. This page describes how RewordAI handles information on reword-ai-six.vercel.app. It is not legal advice.

What you paste

When you click Paraphrase or Summarize, the text in the box is sent to our serverless API, then to an upstream language model so a rewrite can be generated. Do not paste passwords, API keys, health data, or anything you cannot share with a third-party model provider.

Accounts

RewordAI does not require an account. We do not sell a user database because we do not run a signup system on this site.

Logs and hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. Hosting and network logs may include IP address, user agent, and request time. We use them to run the service and to apply a short rate limit on the paraphrase API. We do not use that data to build advertising profiles.

Cookies and ads

This site does not run a third-party ad network. Search engines may crawl public pages. We do not invent analytics numbers on this page.

About RewordAI

RewordAI is a free online paraphrasing tool. Paste a sentence, paragraph, or short draft. Choose a mode. Get a rewrite you can edit.

It is a writing aid. It does not check plagiarism, invent sources, or replace your own work. Names, numbers, and citations stay your responsibility.

What you can do

  • Paraphrase text in Standard, Fluency, Creative, Formal, Shorten, or Expand.
  • Summarize a paragraph with the text summarizer.
  • Rewrite one line with the sentence rewriter or a full paragraph with the paragraph rewriter.

Questions about the product go on the contact page. We do not list a phone number or a private inbox we do not operate.
